<p class="research-lead">
I study how environmental conditions and human activities jointly shape personal exposure histories, with a focus on their measurement, human responses, and population distributions.
</p>

<div class="research-grid">

  <div class="research-item">
    <h3>Exposure estimation</h3>
    <p>Measuring and reconstructing the exposures individuals actually receive in everyday environments.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-item">
    <h3>Exposure–response relationships</h3>
    <p>Understanding how exposure histories relate to health, perception, and behavior.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-item">
    <h3>Exposure distributions</h3>
    <p>Investigating how environmental conditions and human mobility shape population exposures and the benefits of interventions.</p>
  </div>

</div>

<div class="research-methods">
  <h3>Methods</h3>
  <p>Wearable and environmental sensing · Spatiotemporal data fusion · Statistical and machine-learning modelling · Human-subject studies</p>
</div>
